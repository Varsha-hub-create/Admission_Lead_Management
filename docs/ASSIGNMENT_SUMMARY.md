# Assignment 5 — Admission Lead Management

## Business Problem
Educational institutions receive admission leads from websites, walk-ins, phone calls, WhatsApp, fairs and campaigns. Without a centralized process, leads can be missed, follow-ups can become overdue, ownership is unclear and managers lack funnel visibility.

## Proposed Solution
A role-based Admission Lead Management CRM that provides:
1. Multi-channel lead capture
2. Course preference tracking
3. Counsellor assignment
4. Lead lifecycle/status management
5. Follow-up scheduling and history
6. Ageing visibility
7. Manager dashboard
8. Conversion and source reporting
9. CSV export
10. Authentication and access control

## Success Measures
- Every enquiry has an owner or explicit unassigned state.
- Every active lead can have a next action.
- Managers can see funnel volume and conversion.
- Overdue follow-ups are visible.
- Source and course data are available for analysis.
